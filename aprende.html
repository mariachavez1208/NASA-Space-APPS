No difficult words

Learn how we see Earth from space

You don't need to be a scientist to understand this. We explain it step by step.

🛰️

What is NISAR?

It is a satellite built together by NASA (United States) and ISRO (India). It circles the Earth and looks at it again and again to see what changes.

📡

What is radar?

The satellite sends waves toward the ground and listens for the "echo" that comes back, like when you shout in a cave. That's why it can see even when it's cloudy or at night.

🔍

How do changes show up?

A forest, a palm field and bare ground send back different echoes. If the echo from a place changes over the years, we know something happened there.

🇪🇺

And Sentinel-1?

It is a European radar satellite. We use its 2016 images to compare what Izabal looked like before NISAR existed.

Why does it matter in Izabal?

⛏️

Mining

Removing soil and rock changes the ground and can affect the water in the lake and rivers that communities use.

🌴

Oil palm

When the rainforest is replaced by a single type of plant, animals, plants and water sources are lost.

👨‍👩‍👧

Communities

Knowing about these changes helps families take care of their land and make informed decisions.

Words you will find

Satellite

A machine that circles the Earth in space and collects information about it.

SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar)

The type of radar used by NISAR and Sentinel-1. It combines many echoes to form a very detailed image of the ground.

Deforestation

When trees are cut down or burned and the forest disappears.

Wetland

Land that is covered with water for much of the year, like swamps and lake shores. It is home to many animals.

Open data

Information that anyone can see, use and share for free.

Behind the Green

A project for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. Radar data from the NASA-ISRO NISAR mission, explained for everyone.
