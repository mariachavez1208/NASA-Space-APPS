How radar sees Izabal

These images come from the NISAR (2026) and Sentinel-1 (2016) satellites. The colors are not what you would see with your eyes: each color shows a type of surface.

How to read the colors

  • Green: forest and vegetation
  • Dark blue or black: water
  • Yellow and orange: bare ground, crops or towns
  • Pink (NISAR): open land
