Our land

What is happening in Izabal?

Izabal has the largest lake in Guatemala, rainforests, rivers and a Caribbean coast. Activities like mining and oil palm plantations have changed the landscape. Here you can see those changes with your own eyes.

How does it work?

The satellite takes radar "photos"

Radar sees the ground even through clouds or at night, like a bat using echoes.

We compare before and after

By putting together images from different dates, we can see what changed on the ground.

We explain it in your language

We show the changes with simple colors, clear text and read-aloud narration.

Everyone can contribute

Researchers and communities can upload new data so the information keeps growing.
